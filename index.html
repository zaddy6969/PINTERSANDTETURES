A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Something else on your mind?

Speak with our team
Which services do you offer?+

We offer interior and exterior painting, decorative wall textures, wood finishes and wallpaper. You can also share a wall artwork idea with us to discuss its scope.

How do I get a quotation?+

Use “Let’s talk” to share your name, contact number and project details through WhatsApp or email. Our team can then discuss the surfaces, finish, measurements and next steps for a quotation.

Can you recreate a finish from a photograph?+

A reference photograph is a useful starting point. Share the image and we can discuss a similar direction. The final appearance depends on the material, surface, lighting and application; agree a sample where appropriate.

Can textured finishes be used in any room?+

Suitability depends on the particular finish, moisture exposure and surface condition. Tell us where you would like to use it so we can discuss the appropriate options.

How long will the work take?+

Timing depends on the size of the project, preparation, chosen finish and drying time. The schedule is discussed after reviewing your requirements.
