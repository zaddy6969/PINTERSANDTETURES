What is texture paint?

Texture finishes add pattern, tactile detail or dimension. The result depends on the material, technique, lighting and colour.

Can textures be used in every room?

Suitability depends on the product and room conditions. We can help discuss the right finish for your surface.

Should I test a sample first?

Yes. Test it on the intended wall and view it in different lighting before choosing.

What work do you undertake?
