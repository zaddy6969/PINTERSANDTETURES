A few helpful answers

Before you begin.

What is texture paint?

Texture finishes add visible pattern, tactile detail or dimensional effects. The final appearance depends on the material, application technique, lighting and colour.

Can textures be used in every room?

Suitability varies by product and exposure. Confirm the correct finish with the manufacturer or a qualified applicator for each surface.

Should I test a sample first?

Yes. Test it on the intended wall and check it at different times of day alongside your flooring, furniture and lighting.

What types of work do you undertake?

We take on all types of painting work and texture work. Contact us to discuss your specific project requirements.
